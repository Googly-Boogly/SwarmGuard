# Research methodology

RQ1–RQ3 (registry `rq-registry-2`) have registered hypotheses, null hypotheses, controls, confounders and analysis plans. Current measurements are synthetic pipeline validation. The native laboratory supports scripted fixtures, observation-driven mock autonomous policies and recorded replay. Live autonomous laboratory configurations are rejected. Existing Stage 3/5 paid CLI paths remain separately opt-in.

Primary comparisons use paired critical checkpoints. Four general monitors versus four specialized monitors controls council size. Mock responses share deterministic rules; agreement cannot establish philosophical specialization benefits. Gateway outcomes, prohibited synthetic sends, monitor predictions and legitimate completion are separate measurements.

Adaptive search remains restricted to adaptive_search. Dataset imports select fixed trusted templates; they cannot supply labels, tools, identities, resources, policies or code. Public inherited held-out fixtures do not constitute independent blind evidence. VALIDATION currently aliases development templates and must not be represented as an independent partition.
